# Colours

Every colour is a token, used as a Tailwind class: `bg-surface`, `text-muted`, `border-border-strong`, `text-danger`.
Tailwind's own palette does not compile, and lint rejects colour literals, arbitrary values and opacity modifiers
such as `bg-accent/10`: a tint is a colour no contrast check has seen. Each token has a light and a dark value; the
tables show both.

## Surfaces

`background` is the page, `surface` a card, panel or header.

<Story of={Stories.Surfaces} />

## Lines

`border` separates: card edges, rules, table rows. `border-strong` outlines something you can act on, such as a
button, and reaches 3:1 on every surface.

<Story of={Stories.Lines} />

## Text

`foreground` for content, `muted` for secondary text, labels and hints. Both reach 4.5:1 on every surface.

<Story of={Stories.Text} />

## Accent

The brand colour, an indigo: links, the product name and selected text. `accent-foreground` is text on an accent fill. `focus`
is the focus ring; it follows `accent`, and `src/styles/base.css` draws it outside every `:focus-visible` element.

<Story of={Stories.Accent} />

## Status

The outcome of anything: `ok`, `warn`, `danger`. They are text colours (4.5:1) and always come with a word or a mark.

<Story of={Stories.Status} />

## Data

A provider's usage wears the provider's own colour: its mark, its gauge and the line of an account's chart. The
state is carried by the figure instead, which turns amber and then red, each with a mark. An account's row is drawn
over a chart of what it had used: a line with a glow under it that fades towards the foot of the row. The `-track`
colours are the deepest that glow gets, and the row's text is checked against them. `src/lib/providerTones.ts` maps
a provider to its classes. Every provider with a mark wears its brand's colour, stepped lighter or darker only as far
as each theme needs to read it (tokens.css says where each came from); a provider's products share it (Gemini, Gemini
CLI, Antigravity and Vertex are all Google's blue). One whose mark is black and white (OpenCode, Cursor, GitHub
Copilot, xAI, Ollama, Z.ai and others) gets `mono`, and one with no mark gets `meter`. Several brands share a hue
(many are orange or blue), so the mark and name beside a gauge always say whose it is.

<Story of={Stories.Providers} />

The detail chart draws up to three series. Their colours stay apart for colour-blind readers, and none is a status
or provider colour.
